# Task prompts

## Component change

Read the root instructions and current design tokens. Preserve the sidebar/top navigation, central artwork, and music/registration footer. Identify the component's states and data contract, make the requested change, and add meaningful Storybook interactions. Verify the width/height matrix with geometry checks and screenshots. Report what was tested and any unavailable browser or third-party behavior.

## Scene change

Use the established scene reference and deterministic seed. Preserve composition, palette, depth, and the characteristic cube cloud or event-horizon silhouette. Use R3F's lifecycle and existing performance tools. Measure the identified bottleneck under the same conditions before and after changing it. Verify rapid switching, pause, context loss, and resize. Distinguish measured frame intervals from GPU time.

## Review

Inspect the running page and relevant stories. Check small and large widths, short and tall heights, keyboard focus, content extremes, reduced motion, and graphics/network failures. For each issue, provide its location, reproduction, evidence, and a focused fix. Do not redesign the page while reviewing a preservation task.

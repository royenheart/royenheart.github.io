## What the reference can establish

The supplied historical-looking photograph shows pale, long, irregular tracks extending from near the ground through much of the sky, with several tracks cropped by the frame. Their widths and brightness vary; broad bends coexist with smaller roughness. Endpoints do not organize the image. Neighboring tracks appear to belong to a larger surrounding medium.

The exact event and provenance of that attachment have not been established. Do not identify the mushroom cloud or all visible details from appearance alone.

Historical nuclear-test reports describe **smoke tracers**, generated before an event and photographed as a shock passed. This explains the relevant class of long tracks; it does not imply that nuclear radiation is a bundle of visible thin lines. [Defense Nuclear Agency, Operation TEAPOT, page 87](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=cf2552ae9814084640d8faf8e2142b64bf4126ff&repid=rep1&type=pdf).

**Schlieren / background-oriented schlieren** exposes density-related refractive distortion. NASA explains how compressed air changes the apparent position of textured backgrounds. The transferable visual mechanism is a broad disturbance of the scene, with narrow bright traces acting as readable markers. [NASA AirBOS](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/airborne-background-oriented-schlieren-imaging-airbos/).

**Wilson condensation clouds** are another visible phenomenon: suitable moist air can temporarily condense during the decompression phase, producing a cloud or ring rather than isolated glowing lines. This supports distinguishing smoke markers, refractive wavefronts and volumetric clouds. F6 does not add a white condensation dome because it would replace the requested filament character and obscure the golden scene. [Glasstone and Dolan, chapter II, sections 2.48–2.50](https://www.atomicarchive.com/resources/documents/effects/glasstone-dolan/chapter2.html).

F6 borrows qualitative optical cues for the scene transition; it does not simulate an atmospheric explosion.

## Diagnose the rendered candidate

Connected-component measurements on F5's isolated emission at 1440 × 900, fixed time 12, threshold luminance 45 show several side traces occupying only approximately 32–45% of image height at 12% release. Their visible endpoints cluster inside the composition. The nearest trace is longer through perspective, but that does not correct the short lateral traces.

Four choices compound the detached-line reading:

1. A local height near 4.4 units limits vertical continuity.
2. Tapers make each trace read as a separate object with a beginning and end.
3. Independent phases weaken the sense of a common disturbance.
4. Narrow, nearly uniform cores dominate over the wider background distortion.

The measurements are a bounded diagnosis of F5, not an objective measure of artistic quality. Original scripts and results are outside Git under `/tmp/proxy-radial-wave/length-analysis.json`.

## F6 implementation

| Dimension | F5 | F6 refinement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spatial path | Five fan sectors, three delayed radial packets | Preserved |
| Vertical extent | Short local span | 2.85 times the span; prominent endpoints tend to leave the viewport |
| Broad curvature | Separate phase per curve | Shared slow bends in height, with a smaller per-curve residual |
| Surface variation | Smooth, nearly uniform core | Continuous changes in width, density and a fine interior fold |
| Edge | Narrow line with pointed taper | Warm soft shoulder; taper restricted to the outermost 3.5% of the longer span |
| Refraction support | 0.50 local strip half-width | 1.25 local half-width, with a stronger signed displacement |
| Color | Pearl-gold line | Lower-intensity pearl fold plus copper/gold body and shoulder |

This is best described as **long, correlated smoke-like strands with schlieren-inspired refraction**. It is a descriptive art-direction phrase rather than a standardized effect name. The shared broad bend makes neighboring curves respond as a group; finer variation prevents identical copies. No additional full-screen glow mask is added.

The visible and refractive passes still share the exact same world geometry. R3F, Drei and the existing camera retain ownership of the rendering lifecycle. Curve sampling increases from 48 to 96 segments to support the longer span without coarse corners. The visible strip carries procedural density variation rather than a photographic texture or imported nuclear-test asset.

The current 43-degree text/rail rotation introduces a separate reading direction and independent line endpoints. Prioritize a shared shallow inset with two oblique segments and horizontal icon/label content. Keep theme-derived gold/blue accents, IBM Plex Sans, the accepted rounded deck, and Material reflection.
